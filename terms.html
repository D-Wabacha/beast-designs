Terms & service policy

These terms apply to orders placed with BeasT Designs through the Love Cards Kenya website.

What we provide

We provide personalised digital greeting cards as image files, in the formats listed on our help page. We don't currently print or deliver physical cards.

Prices

All prices are in Kenyan shillings (KES) and are shown before you place your order. The total on your order page is the amount to pay. Standard M-PESA transaction charges set by Safaricom are paid by the sender.

Orders and payment

  • Placing an order saves your request and gives you an order reference. It doesn't mean payment has been received.
  • Payment is made through M-PESA as described on your order page. We verify payments by hand, and an order is only treated as paid once we have confirmed the payment.
  • Sharing an M-PESA code doesn't by itself confirm payment.

Your content

You are responsible for the names and messages you add to your card. We may decline orders containing content that is abusive, hateful, harassing or unlawful, or that uses other people's names or images without permission.

Changes and problems

You can ask for corrections to your card before it is marked ready. If there's a problem with your order or payment, contact us on WhatsApp with your order reference and we'll work with you to resolve it.

Design ownership

Card designs are created by BeasT Designs. Your finished card is for your personal use, including sharing it with its recipient and printing it for personal use.
