Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains what information BeasT Designs collects through the Love Cards Kenya website, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

  • Your card content: recipient names, messages, sign-offs and design choices.
  • Your contact details: your name, phone number and, if you give it, your email address.
  • Payment details you share: your M-PESA confirmation code and, optionally, the name on the payment. We never ask for or store your M-PESA PIN.
  • Order notes you choose to add.

Why we use it

Only to make your card, verify your payment, deliver your card, and contact you about your order. We don't sell your information or use your card messages for advertising.

Where it is stored

  • Orders are stored in our order database on the server that runs this website. Only our staff can view them, and only after signing in.
  • Unfinished card designs, and your contact details if you tick "remember my details", are saved only in your own browser on your device. You can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Share links

The "Copy share link" option in the designer puts your card's words into the link itself. Anyone you send it to can see those words. It doesn't include your contact details or order information.

Your order link

Your order page link contains a private key. Anyone who has the link can see your order and card content, so please don't post it publicly.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep a record of a payment. Contact us on WhatsApp or call +254 705 459 618.
