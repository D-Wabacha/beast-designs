How ordering works

Everything you need to know about choosing, personalising, paying for and receiving your card.

How it works

  1. Choose a design

    Browse all designs or filter by occasion. Each design shows its price up front.

  2. Personalise it

    Add the recipient's name, your message and sign-off, then choose colours, lettering, alignment and text size.

  3. Preview it

    The preview updates as you type. Your final card is produced from exactly the same settings, without the faint "preview" mark.

  4. Place your order

    Enter your name and phone number. You'll get an order reference straight away. Nothing is charged at this point.

  5. Pay & receive

    Pay by M-PESA and share your confirmation code. Once we've confirmed the payment, we finish your card and send it to you.

How we verify payments

We check every payment by hand against our M-PESA statement. Your order goes through these stages:

  • Awaiting payment. Your order is saved and waiting for you to pay.
  • Payment being verified. You've shared your M-PESA code and we're checking it.
  • Payment confirmed. We've seen the money arrive.
  • Card ready. Your full-resolution card is ready to download, and we send it on WhatsApp if you chose that option.

We will never ask for your M-PESA PIN. If anyone claiming to be us asks for it, don't share it.

Formats and prices

Card prices are shown on each design. The format price is added on top. There are no delivery fees or hidden charges.

Frequently asked questions

When will I receive my card?

We start on your card once we've confirmed your payment. You can follow its progress on your order page, and we'll message you on WhatsApp if you chose WhatsApp delivery.

Can I see the card before paying?

Yes. The designer shows a live preview of your card. The full-resolution file without the preview mark is released after payment is confirmed.

I paid but my order still says "Payment being verified".

That's normal. We confirm payments by hand, so it can take a little while. If it's been a long time, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference and M-PESA code.

I made a mistake in my message. Can I change it?

Yes, as long as your card hasn't been marked ready. Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference and the correction.

Do you print and deliver physical cards?

Not at the moment. Our cards are digital. If you choose the printable file, you can print it at home or at any print shop.

Can someone else pay for my order?

Yes. When you share the M-PESA code, add the payer's name so we can match the payment.

My occasion isn't listed.

Our open designs, like Wildflower Meadow and Classic Invitation, work for almost anything. For something truly custom, message us on WhatsApp.

I lost my order link.

Go to My order and enter your reference and phone number.
