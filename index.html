Questions

Good to know

What exactly will I receive?

A digital card is a phone-ready PNG image (1200 × 1800 px). A printable file is a higher-resolution PNG (2400 × 3600 px) for printing at home or at a print shop. We don't offer printed or delivered physical cards yet.

When do I get my card?

After we've confirmed your payment, we finish your card and send it on WhatsApp. You can also download it from your order page once it's marked ready.

Can I change my message after ordering?

Yes, as long as your card hasn't been marked ready. Message us on WhatsApp with your order reference.

My occasion isn't listed. Can you help?

Yes. Try the "Just because" or "Classic invitation" designs, which work for almost anything, or message us with what you need.
